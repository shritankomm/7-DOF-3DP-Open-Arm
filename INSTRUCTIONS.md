**Assembly Instructions**


1. 3D Printing


Print all parts using the settings listed in the CAD folder.
For motor-bearing and joint-critical parts use at least 40% gyroid infill and 4 wall loops.
Check the CAD folder for left vs right arm variants before printing the second side.


2. Heatset Inserts


Before assembling anything, go through every printed part and install heatset inserts while parts are still loose.
Any hole with a diameter of .171" gets an M3 heatset insert.
Use a soldering iron at around 200-220C and press each insert in flush or just below the surface.


3. Extrusion and Chest Plate


Attach the extrusion clamps to the aluminum extrusion.
Mount the chest plate onto that assembly and make sure everything is square and tight before moving on.


4. Motor and Joint Assembly


Start from the bottom of the arm and work upward toward the shoulder.
Any hole with a diameter of .126" gets an M3 screw.
Build each joint as a subassembly first, then attach it to the arm. Do not try to assemble everything in place as some screws will be completely unreachable once the joint above is attached.
Work up in this order: Wrist Yaw, Wrist Roll, Wrist Pitch, Elbow Roll, Elbow Pitch, Shoulder Yaw, Shoulder Roll, Shoulder Pitch.
Route servo cables as you go. Do not wait until the arm is fully assembled.
Do a dry fit of each joint before driving any screws to confirm alignment.


5. Second Arm


Repeat steps 3 and 4 for the opposite side.


6. Camera/Wiring


Attach the camera and electronics to the area on the chest plate
