### Before you start — heat set inserts + extra info/tips

- For any part with holes that are 0.157 in with a 0.2 in chamfer, press M3 heat set inserts into them using a soldering iron

<img width="943" height="1162" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e00e997c-fa5e-47ea-8e20-66e58bfbd691" />

- Any part with a hole that is .126 is a M3 screw pilot hole so those will connect to other componenets using m3 screws 

- Any part with .079 is a m2 screw pilot hole so those parts will connect to motors via self tapping screws

- Any screw that has the word Motor in it is a star head and is provided with the motors when purchased so dont use socket head screws for connecting parts to the servo horns.

## Stand and Mounting


<img width="635" height="661" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/380c7c8e-c3d2-426b-acdf-7422907b82d2" />


<img width="668" height="1229" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/868b0bda-b6c0-489a-998f-890a2a47150a" />

Description: This stand is made of wood to keep it cheap and easy to build. First, place two pieces of wood parallel to each other on either side of the 4040, then put a piece of wood in front of the two pieces of wood in such a way that they are touching each other. Then use the self-tapping screws to connect these 3 pieces of wood together to form a T-shape around the 4040 extrusion. It should hold the extrusion tight. Then add the 6-inch piece of wood in the back and use the self-tapping screws to secure it in place, but don't tighten it too much. Just enough so that the piece stays straight up, as when tightened the parallel pieces of wood will converge and make the wood bend.

Fasteners: 20 in 2x4 wood (3x), 6 in 2x4 wood (1x), 3 in #8 self tapping wood screws (8x)

## Wiring

Use the wires and connect each to each other's ports in the back of the servos; make sure the wires in the daisy chain don't hinder the movements of the joint. Connect 8 motors to one of the Waveshare UART ports and another 8 connected to the other UART port to make one long daisy chain.

Both ports on the Waveshare adapter are parallel taps on the same bus, not independent channels. All 16 servos share one bus, which is why the IDs have to be unique across both arms.

Daisy chain each arm servo to servo, then run one 3-prong cable from each arm back to the adapter board. Power the board from the 12 V 10 A supply before connecting USB to the host computer.

---

Once these steps are completed, the arm will be fully assembled. You can now move on to working with software and implementing movement protocols.
